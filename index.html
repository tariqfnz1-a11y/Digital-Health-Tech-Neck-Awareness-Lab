Module 1 · Understand

What is Tech Neck?

Tech Neck is a common term for neck discomfort and muscular strain associated with repeatedly looking down at devices or holding the head in an awkward position for long periods.

01
Neck stiffness

Difficulty or discomfort when turning your head.

02
Shoulder tension

Tightness or fatigue around the neck and shoulders.

03
Headaches

Some people experience headaches associated with neck tension.

Important: Symptoms can have many causes. This learning activity is for awareness, not diagnosis. Persistent, worsening or unusual symptoms should be assessed by a healthcare professional.

Teacher / facilitator area

Suggested 25-minute lesson

5 min · Introduce

Explain Tech Neck, common signs and why posture matters.

5 min · Explore

Ask learners to compare neutral and forward-head positions without forcing either posture.

5 min · Discuss

Use the student, teacher and parent scenarios.

5 min · Assess

Complete the knowledge challenge individually or in pairs.

5 min · Commit

Each learner selects two habits to practise this week.
